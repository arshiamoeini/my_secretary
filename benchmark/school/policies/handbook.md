# Maplewood Elementary - Staff Handbook (excerpt 2026-27)

## 2.1 School hours
School is in session from 8:15 AM to 3:05 PM Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and
Friday. Every Wednesday is an early-release day: students are dismissed at
1:30 PM. The office closes at 4:00 PM.

## 2.2 Medication during school hours
Only the school nurse (or a nurse's designated substitute) may administer
medication to a student. Staff must have (a) a written authorization signed by
the parent or guardian and (b) a written order from the student's physician on
file before any dose is given. Every dose must be recorded in the medication
log with time, dose, and the name of the person who administered it. Students
may self-carry only inhalers and epinephrine auto-injectors, and only with a
completed self-carry authorization.

## 2.3 Attendance and absence reporting
Families must report an absence by 9:00 AM. An absence is excused for illness,
a medical or dental appointment, a family emergency, or a religious observance.
All other absences are unexcused. After three unexcused absences in a term the
office sends a warning letter; after six it refers the family to the district
attendance officer.

## 2.4 Student records and privacy (including phone requests)
Student records are confidential. When someone telephones about a student,
office staff may confirm only whether the student is present or absent, and
only after verifying the caller's identity against the guardian on file (name
plus the student's date of birth). Staff must never disclose the reason for an
absence, grades, health or allergy information, or contact details by phone.
Requests for records from outside agencies must go to the principal.

## 2.5 Field trips
A signed permission slip must be on file at least five school days before a
field trip. Slips are trip-specific: a slip signed for a different trip or a
different school year is not valid.

## 2.6 Visitors
All visitors sign in at the front office, show photo ID, and wear a visitor
badge. Volunteers must have a completed background check on file.

## 2.7 Room bookings
Rooms are booked through the office. A room may not be double-booked; the
earlier booking has priority and the later requester must be rebooked.
